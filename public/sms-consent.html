Text Message (SMS) Consent & Opt-In Policy

This page documents how members and visitors of our church consent to receive text messages, in compliance with carrier requirements.

How people opt in

  • Paper Connect Card: at our services, members and visitors complete a paper Connect Card where they provide their mobile number and agree to be contacted by the church.
  • Membership database entry: church staff add a person's mobile number to our membership database only after that person has agreed, in person, to be contacted.

Consent is always collected in person. We never buy, rent, sell, or share phone numbers with anyone.

Consent statement shown to members & visitors

"By providing your phone number to New Testament Christian Church of Glendale, you agree to receive informational and pastoral text messages — including service and event reminders, follow-up and care messages, prayer requests, and announcements. Message frequency varies. Message & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help."

What we send

  • Service and event reminders
  • Visitor and member follow-up / pastoral care
  • Prayer requests and church announcements
  • Occasional administrative notices
Non-marketing · Congregation communication only

Opting out

Recipients can opt out at any time by replying STOP. For assistance, reply HELP. We honor all opt-out requests immediately.
